Intro offer questions

Who is the $99 intro offer for?

New clients only. It gives you two weeks of unlimited small-group reformer classes so you can find the classes and instructors you love.

Not at all. We work with complete beginners every day — start with a beginner-friendly Level 1 class and build from there.

Unlimited over the two weeks, with a maximum of one class per day.

When does the intro offer expire?

Your two weeks begin on your first booked class, and the intro package must be used within 21 days of purchase.

What should I bring?

Comfortable activewear and grip socks, which are available for purchase at the studio. Arrive 5–10 minutes early for your first visit.
